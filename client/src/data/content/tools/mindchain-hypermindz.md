It runs a buyer agent (BuyerAgentZ) that routes one brief across an agency's seats, direct deals and agent-to-agent paths, and a seller agent (SellerAgentZ) that negotiates to a publisher's price floors. It has adapters for Google Ad Manager, Index Exchange, Media.net, Equativ, BidSwitch and Nexxen, signs every deal cryptographically, and charges subscriptions plus a flat fee per deal rather than a share of spend. If you run Google, Meta or TikTok campaigns yourself, it's far more machinery than you need, and MCP access is granted per organization on request.
