## FAQ

**How much does PlentyLabs cost?** Pro is €13 a month and Business is €24 a month, both billed yearly with 100 credits a month and unlimited users. Enterprise is custom.

**Does PlentyLabs have a free plan or trial?** The pricing page doesn't list one. PlentyLabs has offered free credits in LinkedIn campaigns, so it's worth asking on a demo call.

**What can you make with PlentyLabs?** Images, video, voice and music, using models such as GPT Image, Nano Banana, FLUX, Veo, Kling and ElevenLabs. You can split images into editable layers and produce one design in many sizes and languages at once.

**Does PlentyLabs connect to Meta or TikTok Ads?** Not according to its site. You export the creatives and upload them yourself, and it doesn't see how they perform.

**Does PlentyLabs work with Claude or ChatGPT?** Yes. It has an MCP server and a GraphQL API, so an AI client can work with your canvas. The developer docs don't say which plans include it.

**Who owns PlentyLabs?** It has been part of Epidemic Group, alongside Epidemic Sound, since April 2026. It started in Stockholm in 2025 as VXA.

**Is PlentyLabs the same as PlentyONE or plentymarkets?** No. PlentyONE (formerly plentymarkets) is a German e-commerce ERP. PlentyLabs makes AI ad creative.
